Privacy Policy — PhuTungOto

App com.daitrandev.phutungoto (Android & iOS)
Last updated: 22 September 2026

PhuTungOto is a car parts lookup app built and operated by the Phu Tung Xe Hoi auto parts store. This page explains what data the app collects, why it is collected, where it is sent, and how you can have it deleted.

In short: The app needs your phone number to sign you in, and your contact details when you send an order request. It shows ads served by Google AdMob. Photos you take with the camera are never uploaded anywhere. We do not sell your data to anyone.

1. Data we collect

1.1 Information you provide

DataWhenWhy we need it
Phone number and password When signing in To authenticate your account. Accounts are issued by the store; the app has no sign-up screen.
Full name, delivery address When you fill them in on the Account screen To pre-fill your details on future order requests.
Contact phone number, full name, address, notes When you send an order request So the store can call you back with a quote and arrange delivery. Guests who are not signed in can also send requests.
Cart contents (part code, quantity, vehicle name, colour) When you send an order request This is the content of your order.

1.2 Information the app records automatically

DataDescriptionWhy we need it
VIN numbers you look up Sent to our server to query the manufacturer catalogue. Results are retained on the server. To return lookup results and to serve repeat lookups faster.
Part codes you search for Sent to our server when you search. To return search results.
Device identifiers A random value generated by the app at install time, plus an identifier the operating system scopes to this app alone. These are not your IMEI, serial number or phone number, and are not used to identify you outside this app. To limit how many devices can share one account, and to block devices flooding the server with requests.
Device model name For example "Samsung SM-S921B". Sent when you sign in. Shown in your "Signed-in devices" list so you can recognise your own devices.
Advertising identifier Collected by Google AdMob, not by us. See section 5. To serve ads.

1.3 Data that stays on your device

The following is stored in the app's private storage on your device and is not sent to our server, except where stated otherwise:

  • The cart you are building. If you are signed in, your cart is backed up to the server so you can reopen it on another device — this backup is overwritten each time you change the cart.
  • Your VIN lookup history (last 10 entries) and recent search terms.
  • The contact details you entered on your last order, used to pre-fill the next one.
  • How many free lookups you have used in the past 24 hours (applies to guests who are not signed in).
  • Your light/dark mode choice and other interface settings.

Uninstalling the app erases everything in this section.

2. What we do NOT collect

  • GPS location.
  • Contacts, messages or call logs.
  • Your photo library — the app only reads the single image you deliberately pick for code scanning, and does not upload it anywhere.
  • Audio or microphone input.
  • The app integrates no third-party analytics or crash-reporting tools whatsoever (no Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Sentry or similar).

3. Camera and label scanning

The app requests camera permission so you can photograph a part label and extract the code printed on it.

Images never leave your device. Text recognition runs entirely on-device using an image-processing library bundled with the app. Only the recognised string of characters (for example 51712-T5A-000) is sent to our server to be searched — exactly as if you had typed that code on the keyboard yourself.

Captured images are stored temporarily in the app's own directory for processing. You can remove them at any time by clearing the app's data in your system settings, or by uninstalling the app.

4. How we use your data

  • To sign you in and keep you signed in.
  • To return parts lookup results by VIN, by vehicle or by part code.
  • To pass your order request to the store so staff can call you back with a quote.
  • To enforce free lookup limits and to block devices sending floods of requests.
  • To display ads (see section 5).

We do not use your data to build advertising profiles, and we do not sell or rent your data to third parties.

5. Advertising

The app displays ads through Google AdMob. AdMob is a Google service and may collect your device's advertising identifier along with usage data in order to select relevant ads. That collection is performed by Google, under the Google privacy policy.

You have the following choices:

  • On iOS: the app asks whether you allow tracking. If you decline, ads still appear but are non-personalised.
  • On Android: go to Settings → Google → Ads to delete your advertising ID or turn off ad personalisation.
  • Premium accounts issued by the store show no ads at all.

6. Data sharing

Your data goes only to the following places:

  • Our server at phutung4banh.id.vn — which stores accounts, order requests and lookup data.
  • Store staff — who can see your order requests (contact details and the list of parts) in order to call you back with a quote.
  • Google AdMob — as described in section 5.

We may also disclose data where required by a lawful request from a competent government authority under Vietnamese law.

7. Storage and security

  • All traffic between the app and our server uses HTTPS.
  • Your password is not stored on your device. The app only keeps a session token so you do not have to sign in every time you open it.
  • Accounts, order requests and lookup history are retained on the server for as long as the account is active, or until you ask us to delete them.
  • Signing out removes the session token from your device. You can also sign another device out remotely under Account → Signed-in devices.

8. Your rights

  • Access and correction: your full name and delivery address can be edited directly on the Account screen in the app.
  • Deleting on-device data: clear the app's data in your system settings, or uninstall the app.
  • Deleting your account and server-side data: because accounts are issued by the store, deletion is handled manually. Send a request by email or Zalo using the details in section 11, including the phone number you sign in with. We will delete the account, its profile, its cart backup and its lookup history within 30 days. Order requests that resulted in a completed transaction may be retained for reconciliation and accounting obligations.

9. Children

The app is a professional tool for mechanics and car owners. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. If you believe your child has provided us with data, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes we update this page and revise the date at the top. Significant changes to how we handle data will also be announced inside the app.
