This page explains how to request deletion of your PhuTungOto account and the data associated with it. You do not need to sign in to read or use these instructions.

Note: PhuTungOto has no sign-up screen. Accounts are issued by our store to its customers, so deletion is handled by our staff rather than by a self-service button. Every request below is answered.

How to request deletion

Use whichever option is easiest. All three reach the same people.

  1. From inside the app: open the menu at the top left, tap Tài khoản (Account), then Xoá tài khoản (Delete account). Confirm the dialog and our support chat opens with your request.
  2. By email: send a message to tranhieudai13@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my PhuTungOto account".
  3. By Zalo: message 0338867216.

In your message, include the phone number you use to sign in. That is how we locate your account. We may ask one question to confirm the account is yours before erasing anything.

What happens next

  • We confirm receipt of your request.
  • Your account is deleted within 30 days of the request.
  • We notify you once the deletion is complete.

What is deleted

DataResult
Your account and sign-in credentialsDeleted permanently
Your profile: full name and delivery addressDeleted permanently
Your saved cart backupDeleted permanently
Your VIN and part number lookup historyDeleted permanently
Your signed-in device listDeleted permanently

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. After it completes, you can no longer sign in, and your lookup history and saved cart cannot be recovered.

What is kept, and for how long

DataRetentionWhy
Order requests that led to a completed sale Up to 10 years Required for accounting and tax records under Vietnamese law. Kept as a transaction record of the sale, not as an active customer profile.
Anonymised, aggregated statistics Indefinitely Counts and totals only. Contains nothing that identifies you.

Order requests that never led to a sale are deleted along with your account.

Deleting data without deleting your account

If you would rather keep your account but remove what is stored on your device, clear the app's data in your phone's system settings, or uninstall the app. That erases your local cart, lookup history, recent searches and saved contact details. It does not affect data held on our servers — use the request options above for that.
